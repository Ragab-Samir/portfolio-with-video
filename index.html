Retail · Profit Leakage Diagnostic

Superstore Sales & Profitability Diagnostic

images/superstore.png
  • Which sub-categories are profitable on paper but losing money once discounting is factored in?
  • At what discount threshold does margin turn negative, and does that threshold vary by category?
  • Which customers and regions are driving the bulk of profit, versus just the bulk of sales volume?
  • Profit Margin % = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)
  • Discounted Orders = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(Orders), Orders[Discount] > 0)
  • Power Query: standardized date formats, removed duplicate order lines, and split the Customer table out of the flat CSV extract.

Banking · Liquidity Management

Nile Bank: Retail Portfolio & Liquidity Analytics

images/nilebank.png
  • Which branches are net depositing versus net withdrawing this year, and is that shifting month over month?
  • How concentrated is the deposit base — what share sits with the top 10 branches and top customers?
  • How does account-type mix (current, savings, fixed deposit, youth, business) differ by region?
  • Net Cash Flow = [Total Deposits] - [Total Withdrawals]
  • Avg Balance per Customer = DIVIDE([Total Customer Balances], DISTINCTCOUNT(Customers[CustomerID]))
  • Deposit / Withdrawal Ratio = DIVIDE([Total Deposits], [Total Withdrawals])
  • Power Query: consolidated separate customer, branch, and transaction extracts into one relational model joined on a shared branch key.

B2B Distribution · Quota Tracking

Global B2B Sales & Territory Performance Analytics

images/b2b.png
  • How dependent is total revenue on the Bikes category, and what's the exposure if that softens?
  • Which territories are growing year-over-year versus flat, and how does that map to rep coverage?
  • Is revenue concentration among the top 2 reps (48%) a coverage risk worth flagging to sales leadership?
  • YoY Growth % = DIVIDE([Revenue CY] - [Revenue PY], [Revenue PY])
  • Rep Revenue Share = DIVIDE([Revenue by Rep], CALCULATE([Total Revenue], ALL(SalesReps)))
  • Quota Attainment % = DIVIDE([Actual Revenue], [Quota Target])
  • Power Query: unpivoted quarterly territory extracts, deduplicated rep IDs, and mapped inconsistent country labels to a shared geography table feeding a star schema.
